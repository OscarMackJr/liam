# Phase 02.1 Plan 03: Stub fixture and test helpers Summary

The stub listener now behaves like nono-proxy reverse routes: origin-form `/svc/hometown` and `/svc/olive` (boundary-checked) are forwarded with sentinel Authorization injected on the forwarded hop; any other path gets 502 plus a recorded "unroutable path" error; CONNECT is counted (`connect_attempts`) and answered 405 without forwarding. `ProxyStats` gained `connect_attempts`, `paths`, `header_names`; old fields kept. `gateway_attempt` is a direct request to `http://gateway.invalid/` through the single no-proxy client (fails at deny-all resolver, `resolver_calls() == 1`). `with_gateway_trap` keeps its signature; the trap is never routed to.
